## Card 1: How we make money

- [Built] **Free core.**
  The gateway, rules and audit log stay free (MIT).
- [Next] **Hosted dashboard.**
  Shared rules and sealed logs across offices. Planned: **₹499** per office a month.³
- [Built] **Sector rule packs.**
  HIPAA Safe Harbor ships today. Finance, HR and education come next.

## Card 2: Why now, and who pays

- **Every data fiduciary, by 13 May 2027.**
  That's anyone deciding how personal data is used.²
- **Small offices first.**
  Clinics, CA firms and colleges, with no IT team.
- **Hard to copy.**
  Local-first by design and made for India, with a **hand-labeled** test set others would have to rebuild.

## Card 3: How we reach them [Next]

1. **Start next door.**
   Our own college office, then clinics we can visit in Mangaluru and Udupi.
2. **Go through associations.**
   Associations of CAs and doctors reach many small offices at once.
3. **Let open source spread it.**
   Developers set it up for offices they help.
