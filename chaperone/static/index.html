    Chaperone sits between your team and an AI chatbot. Before a message leaves, it finds names, IDs and sensitive details, applies your rules for this flow, and tries to work out who and what the message is about. The reply comes back with the real values put in again.

    Pick a scenario, or type below. The flow at the top decides what may leave: who sends it, where it goes, and why.
