What happens when you press Check and send

Whether a detail is sensitive depends on the flow: who sends it, where it goes, and why. The same sentence can be fine for an internal tool and a leak to an outside AI.

  1. 1Detect, on this laptop

    Patterns with checksums find Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN and phone numbers. A name model finds people and companies. A small local AI spots health, pay, HR, legal and unpublished results.

  2. 2Decide by flow

    Rules look at the sender, the destination and the purpose. Every decision names the rule that made it.

  3. 3Send only the safe version

    Names become placeholders, details become vague, or the request is blocked. A last check refuses to send any raw value.

  4. 4Put the real names back

    The AI answers with placeholders. They are swapped back to real names on this laptop.
